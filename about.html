ABOUT

In this archive I analyse contemporary issues from a Marxist lens, I consider the implications of an increasingly volatile society for the revolutionary struggle. My focus is on applying a collection of Marxist thinkers works to answer on issues that they did not have to consider in their time period. I choose to distance the majority of my writings from my own personal leftist beliefs as I do not believe the factionalism that is ever so prevalent in the left as a progressive force but rather see it as a divisive one. Despite this I do hold very strong beliefs about differing figures on the left, as is human nature for the Marxist, but refuse to let these beliefs interfere with my writings and attempt to consider the most strands as I can, all would be impossible due to plethora of division amongst the left.

I will occasionally publish essays and notes on this archive with the goal of at least one essay every 2 months and notes much more regularly. The aim of this archive is to collectivise (pun intended) and document my thoughts on issues in a consistent written format rather than committing everything to memory.
